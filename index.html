Apple Watch

Press Set, Pause / Resume and End from your watch while an activity is running. The phone buttons keep working too. Choose the machine and save the activity on the phone as before.

1. Your watch key

Make a key once. It lets the watch shortcut press the buttons for you only. Making a new key stops the old one.

2. Web address

3. Build the shortcut (once, on the iPhone)

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, name it RepLog.
  2. Add List with four items: Set, Pause, End, Status.
  3. Add Choose from List (it uses the List).
  4. Add Get Contents of URL. Paste the web address. Tap ▸ / Show More: Method POST, Request Body JSON, then add three Text fields:
    action = watch
    key = paste your watch key
    cmd = tap and pick Chosen Item
  5. Add Show Result (it shows the answer, such as “✓ Set 2 done · resting”).
  6. Tap the ⓘ / settings for the shortcut and turn on Show on Apple Watch.
  7. On the watch, open Shortcuts → RepLog. To get there in one tap, add the Shortcuts complication to your watch face and pick RepLog.

Each press takes a second or two and needs the phone nearby or Wi-Fi. When you pick up the phone, the timer catches up with what you pressed on the watch.
